01 • About

The person behind the maps.

I am Tanu Pawar, a postgraduate student pursuing an M.Sc. in Geoinformatics. My academic interests lie in the integration of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, Spatial Data Science, Urban Planning, Environmental Monitoring and Geospatial Technologies for solving real-world problems.

I am interested in transforming spatial data into meaningful information that supports evidence-based decision-making. Through coursework, fieldwork, research and project-based learning, I have developed practical experience in spatial analysis, mapping, image interpretation, geospatial programming and Web GIS.

I believe maps are more than representations of space: they can reveal patterns, communicate change and support sustainable development.

Career direction

My goal is to build a career as a GIS and Geospatial Professional with interests in cartography, map making, feature extraction, digitization, geospatial visualization, urban planning and environmental applications.
