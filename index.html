01 · About

The person behind the maps

I am a postgraduate student in M.Sc. Geoinformatics at the Institute of Environment Education and Research, Bharati Vidyapeeth (2025–2027), building on a B.A. in Geography.

My interests sit where GIS, Remote Sensing, Spatial Data Science, Urban Planning and Environmental Monitoring meet. I believe maps are powerful tools for understanding patterns, predicting change and supporting sustainable development.

I aim to become a GIS and Remote Sensing professional designing geospatial solutions for environmental management, urban planning, disaster management and sustainable development.
